DEV

For Developers: 15-Second High-Impact Updates

1. Speak Naturally in English, Hindi, or Hinglish: Loglet is built to code-switch mid-sentence seamlessly. Feel free to say "Worked on Task 4022 kal DB deadlock fix ho gaya..."

2. Say Ticket Numbers Out Loud: Whenever you mention numbers (e.g. Task 12500 or PR #42), Loglet guarantees they will be formatted cleanly into **Task - 12500**.

3. Enable GitSync Radar: Simply type your GitHub username on the home page. Loglet queries your public/repo commits from the last 12 hours so your code literally writes half your update.

4. Build your CareerVault: Every daily update generates a daily Impact Score and catalogs your architectural wins. End of quarter, click 1-button to generate your Promotion Portfolio!

LEAD

For Engineering Managers & Tech Leads

1. 0-Minute Standups ($120k+ Savings): Replace lengthy 30-minute syncs. Team members submit their 30-second audio updates by 5:30 PM.

2. Executive Sprint Health Digest (Loglet Pulse): At 6:00 PM, the Aggregator Agent synthesizes all updates into a single executive briefing featuring 🔴 Critical Blockers, 🟡 Risk Watch, and 🟢 Shipped Items.

3. Easy Team Onboarding: Go to the Teams page, enter your project name & target team size, and send the generated invite link to your team via Slack or Teams.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Where is my team data stored?
Loglet stores all team updates, digests, and brag docs on Supabase (PostgreSQL) with enterprise-grade encryption.

Q: Will my task or ticket numbers get dropped?
No. Explicit task detection guarantees every ticket number is preserved and highlighted in standard markdown formatting.
